## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-26

### Added

- Initial public research release.
- Periodic calendar graph based on a Cartesian product of cycle graphs.
- Sparse PCA loadings with L1 and graph total-variation penalties.
- Alternating optimization with a PD3O loading subproblem.
- PCA-based initialization.
- Model fitting, transformation, inverse transformation, and reconstruction.
- Optimization diagnostics, degeneracy checks, and conditioning diagnostics.
- Stability-analysis utilities.
- Self-contained synthetic demonstration.
- Automated tests for the graph and estimator.
- Mathematical method documentation and citation metadata.
